    Questions

    Good to know.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There's no trial, no account and nothing to unlock. Open it and build.

    Do I need to install anything?

    No. It runs in any modern browser on Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS, iOS and Android. If you like, you can add it to your home screen or dock so it opens like an app.

    How accurate is the simulation?

    Circuits are solved with ngspice, a full SPICE simulator, using models of real parts like the 2N3904 transistor and the NE555 timer. It's the same kind of maths used to design real electronics, so what you see is what the circuit would do.

    Can I save my circuits?

    Your work is kept in your browser between visits. You can also save a circuit to a file, or share it as a link that contains the entire circuit.

    Does it work without internet?

    Yes. After the first visit everything it needs is stored on your device, so it keeps working on a plane or in a classroom with patchy wifi.
